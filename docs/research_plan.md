# Research plan

## Central research question

Does Lunar New Year timing contain incremental out-of-sample forecasting information for China's quarterly retail sales after accounting for standard seasonal time-series dynamics?

## Planned stages

0. **Repository bootstrap** — establish a reproducible repository structure and preserve the supplied project materials.
1. **Data audit and analysis dataset** — document sources and construct one analysis-ready quarterly dataset.
2. **EDA and stationarity** — examine the series, transformations, stationarity, and ACF/PACF.
3. **Forecast evaluation framework** — define leak-free rolling-origin evaluation and simple benchmarks.
4. **Seasonal ARIMA baseline** — establish a seasonal ARIMA benchmark.
5. **Lunar New Year value** — evaluate SARIMA with a Lunar New Year timing variable against the baseline.
6. **Extensions and robustness** — consider PMI, ETS/Theta comparisons, and robustness analysis.
7. **Final reporting** — produce forecasts, interpretation, figures, tables, and the report.

Model orders and final specifications have not been selected.
